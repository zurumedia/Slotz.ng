How it works for buyers

  1. Pick a ticket

    Choose the ticket type and how many you need on the event page.

  2. Pay online

    Pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD through Paystack, a licensed payment provider.

  3. Show your QR

    Save the QR ticket on your phone and show it at the gate to get in.

For organizers

Student groups, departments, promoters, and clubs use Slotz to sell tickets without chasing payment proof in DMs.

  • Sell online

    Share one link on WhatsApp and Instagram. Buyers pay without messaging you.

  • Confirmed payments only

    A ticket is issued only after the payment is confirmed, so fake screenshots do not work.

  • Scan at the gate

    Every QR ticket works once, so the same ticket cannot be used twice.

  • Simple pricing

    Slotz adds a small service fee per ticket at checkout. Organizers pay nothing upfront.

Terms and refunds

Tickets

Each QR ticket admits the number of people shown on it and can be scanned once. Keep your ticket private, because anyone who scans it first gets in.

Refunds

Tickets are not refundable once bought, unless the event is cancelled or the organizer agrees to a refund. If an event is cancelled, buyers are refunded the ticket price. The service fee may be non-refundable.

Problems with a payment

If you were charged but did not get a ticket, contact us with your payment reference and we will fix it.

Privacy

We collect your name and email only to issue your ticket and handle support. We do not sell your information.
