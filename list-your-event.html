Read the organizer terms
  1. What Slotz does. Slotz lists your event and sells tickets online for you. You stay responsible for running the event.
  2. Authority and accuracy. You are the organizer, or you have permission from them, and everything you submit is true.
  3. Prices and fees. You set the ticket prices. Slotz adds a service fee of ₦200 per ticket at checkout, paid by the buyer. Payment processing charges from our payment provider are deducted from ticket sales before we pay you.
  4. Payout. We pay the ticket money, less processing charges, to the account above within 3 business days after your event, with a report of tickets sold.
  5. Entry. You will admit everyone with a valid Slotz ticket. We give you the list of paid tickets, with names, ticket types, and payment references, to check at the gate.
  6. Cancellations and changes. If your event is cancelled, moved, or changed in a big way, you will tell us straight away. We will refund buyers from the ticket money, and you will cover any shortfall.
  7. Refunds. Otherwise, tickets are not refundable.
  8. Information. We may publish your event name, date, venue, poster, and prices. We use buyers' details only for tickets and entry, and we do not sell them.
  9. Removal. We may remove an event that is misleading, unsafe, or unlawful.
